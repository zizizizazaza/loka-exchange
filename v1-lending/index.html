Features

Six mechanisms built for GPU equipment financing

Illustrative

Rent repays investors first

The offtaker pays rent straight into the collection contract, which pays out in a fixed order. The payment address is set in a three-party agreement and no single party can change it.

Rent received this monthCollection contract
1Investors’ amount dueFirst
2Platform service feeSecond
3Compute operatorRemainder

If rent falls short of the first line, no fee is taken and the operator receives nothing that month.

The operator takes the first loss

80%maximum loan-to-value

Borrowing is capped at 80% of the equipment’s appraised value, so the first 20% of any fall in value is borne by the operator, not investors.

Rules are fixed at listing

Release conditions and the payout order are written into the contract when a project is listed. The platform only confirms whether conditions are met; it cannot redirect funds.

No equity for operators

Operators repay monthly from offtake rent, with the equipment held in a project SPV.

Process

From term sheet to monthly repayment

  1. 01

    Term sheet

    Appraise the equipment, agree the amount and terms, and set up the project SPV.

  2. 02

    Subscribe and escrow

    Investor funds go into the project escrow contract until every release condition is met.

  3. 03

    Verify and release

    Monitoring confirms the equipment is online and running. Once all conditions are met, escrow releases funds to the project SPV.

  4. 04

    Monthly repayment

    Rent flows into the collection contract and repays investors first.

Questions

Can’t find an answer? .

No. Funds move from investors through the escrow contract to the project SPV. The platform never holds them.

From rent the offtaker pays each month under the offtake contract. Rates are tiered by the quality of the offtake contract and will be published once the first project’s terms are set.

In the first version, certificates are not transferable and are held to maturity. If a raise is not filled, or release conditions are not met by the deadline, subscriptions are returned.

Operators with GPUs already installed and earning under a signed offtake contract with at least 24 months remaining, borrowing no more than 80% of the equipment’s appraised value, equipment connected to monitoring, and an offtaker willing to sign the three-party agreement.

Each month, rent received is divided by the amount due. Below 1.15, a 30-day cure period begins and the operator must add funds or prepay part of the principal.

If the cure fails, the servicing partner enforces the lien and sells the equipment. Proceeds go to investors first.

A service fee. How it is charged will be set out here once confirmed.

Equipment financing carries risks, including lower revenue, offtaker default, equipment depreciation and losses on sale. Investors may lose principal.
