Verified before it lists

Every asset passes its class’s release conditions before it trades. For compute credit there are six, each signed by a named party and recorded on-chain.

  • Fully subscribed
  • SPV formed, equipment pledged
  • Lien filed
  • Rent routed to collection contract
  • Equipment verified online
  • Loan ≤ 80% of appraisal

Order books with quotes on both sides

A liquidity vault quotes buy and sell orders, so you can sell before maturity. Market prices can be below NAV or par.

Spread

Two vaults

The credit vault earns from rent across many loans. The liquidity vault earns from spreads on the order books.

Rent pays holders first

Buyers pay rent into a collection contract. Holders are paid first, then the service fee, then the operator.

Process

How it works

Trading
  1. 01

    Deposit

    USDC is minted 1:1 into AIUSD, the account currency.

  2. 02

    Trade or earn

    Buy assets on the order book, or deposit into a vault.

  3. 03

    Get paid or sell

    Receive monthly repayments, or sell before maturity.

Listing
  1. 01

    Apply

    Submit the equipment and the signed offtake contract.

  2. 02

    List

    After review and appraisal, the asset lists and investors subscribe into escrow.

  3. 03

    Release

    Once all six conditions are on-chain, funds go to your SPV.

  4. 04

    Repay

    Rent repays holders each month; the rest comes to you.
