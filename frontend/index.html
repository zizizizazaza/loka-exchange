Protection

How your money is protected

Six safeguards, each enforced by a contract, a signed record or a legal claim on the GPUs.

Sample listing NH200-1

Rent never touches the borrower

The AI company pays rent into a controlled account that can only send it on-chain. The collection contract pays out in an order fixed at listing, and nobody can change it afterwards.

Rent covers what holders are owed 1.30 times. Below 1.15 times, a 30-day cure period starts.

Six proofs before a dollar moves

Your money waits in escrow until six conditions are signed by named parties and recorded on-chain. If one is missing at the deadline, everyone is refunded.

  • Fully subscribed
  • SPV formed, GPUs pledged
  • Lien filed
  • Rent routed to contract
  • GPUs verified online
  • Loan ≤ 80% of appraisal

Losses hit the operator first

Loans are capped at 80% of an independent appraisal, so the operator’s own 20% absorbs a fall in value first. Three months of payments are held in reserve from day one.

1Operator equity 2Reserve 3Holders

If the cure period fails, the lien is enforced and the GPUs are re-rented or sold. Proceeds go to holders first.

We can’t move your money

No pooled funds sit with the platform. Escrow and payouts run on contracts, vaults are run by licensed third parties, and the platform cannot redirect funds.

Exit without waiting

Sell your position on the order book at any time instead of waiting for the loan to end. Market makers quote both sides; prices can be below par.

    Process

    How it works

    For traders

    3 steps

    1. Deposit

      USDC is minted 1:1 into AIUSD, the account currency.

    2. Trade or earn

      Buy assets on the order book, or deposit into a vault.

    3. Get paid or sell

      Receive monthly repayments, or sell before maturity.

    Launch app

    For issuers

    4 steps

    1. Apply

      Submit the equipment and the signed offtake contract.

    2. List

      After review and appraisal, the asset lists and investors subscribe into escrow.

    3. Release

      Once all six conditions are on-chain, funds go to your SPV.

    4. Repay

      Rent repays holders each month; the rest comes to you.
